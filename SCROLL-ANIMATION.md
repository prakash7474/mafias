# Scroll-driven camera movement

The canvas stays fixed while the page scrolls through three cinematic chapters. Scroll progress is passed to the R3F scene; the camera moves deeper into the graveyard while red smoke and bats continue animating.

Run `npm install` then `npm run dev`. Tune camera travel in `src/scene/GraveyardScene.jsx` and scroll sections in `src/App.jsx` / `src/styles.css`.
